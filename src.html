Swipe XR Vision Pro · Muse

Drag across the letters to swipe a word. Tap a letter to type it.
  • Gaze + pinch: look at the first letter, pinch, drag your hand through the word, release.
  • Muse: touch the tip to the floating keyboard and glide, or press the tip/side button while pointing. Squeeze (secondary button) drops the keyboard at the pen tip, so you can lay it on a desk and write on the real surface.
  • ⌖ recenters in front of you. ⌫ after a swipe deletes the whole word. Tap a suggestion to swap the last word.
